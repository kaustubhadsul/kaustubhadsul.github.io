About

I'm a thermal engineer who works where lab research meets real deployment. My work at UT Arlington's EMNSPC Lab covers two-phase and single-phase immersion cooling, indirect evaporative cooling, liquid cold plates and thermal interface materials, combining hands-on experimental characterization with CFD in ANSYS Fluent, IcePak and Flownex.

I've taken that work into the field, leading on-site deployment and commissioning of a 10 MW two-phase immersion cooling site. I'm looking to grow into facility-scale cooling leadership.
